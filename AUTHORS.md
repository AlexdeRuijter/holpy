# Authors and contributors

## Original author

**Bohua Zhan** <bzhan@ios.ac.cn>

Designed and implemented holpy: the higher-order logic kernel, base logic,
standard data types, parser/printer, proof-state server, automation, and the
bundled theory library. The original web application and the integral,
geometry, paraverifier, and imperative modules are also his work.

## Maintainer

**Alex de Ruijter** <noreply.alderuijter@gmail.com>

PyPI packaging, `holpy.*` namespace migration, build and test infrastructure,
and ongoing maintenance. The original author approved this direction
(publishing on PyPI and streamlining the distribution).
